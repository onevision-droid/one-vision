# Content Strategy (One Vision)

## 1. Editorial Voice
- **Evidence over claims**: Quantify impact only when the organization can substantiate it (e.g., "Distributed 500 meals" instead of "Changed thousands of lives").
- **Local by default**: Use Imphal/Manipur context, local voices, familiar places, and relevant languages.
- **People before promotion**: Content should prioritize answering real user needs rather than functioning as organizational marketing.

## 2. Programme & Campaign Grammar
Every programme or campaign listed on the site MUST answer:
- **Purpose**: Why it exists.
- **Audience**: Who it serves.
- **Location**: Specific operational area.
- **Current Status**: Active, Paused, or Completed.
- **Tangible Action**: What One Vision actually does.
- **Participation**: How someone can join or request help.

## 3. Ethical Storytelling
- Obtain explicitly informed consent before publishing a story.
- Avoid unnecessary identifying details if they put subjects at risk.
- Avoid humiliating or sensationalizing imagery.
- Subjects retain agency over how they are represented.
- Never fabricate quotes or outcomes.

## 4. UX Copy
- **Action is Obvious**: Link and button text must be descriptive (e.g., "Submit Help Request" rather than "Click Here").
- **No Dark Patterns**: No deceptive donation defaults, forced newsletter opt-ins, or manipulative urgency (e.g., countdown timers for relief funds).
